# Problem solving: Atomic Skills

Frame ambiguous problems and turn them into options, decisions, plans, or critiques.

## Subcategories

- `category-decision-review` — **Decision and review**: Compare options against criteria or critique an artifact against an explicit rubric.
- `category-ideation-planning` — **Ideation and planning**: Generate distinct options and convert a selected direction into an executable plan.
- `category-problem-framing` — **Problem framing**: Clarify goals, constraints, stakeholders, assumptions, and success criteria before solving.
