# Business and Growth Router

1. Identify the decision, market, time horizon, evidence standard, and risk level.
2. Read the locale-appropriate Atomic Skill index in `references/`.
3. Compose research or data atoms explicitly instead of duplicating them inside broad marketing Skills.
4. Escalate financial, legal, or external-communication authority through the declared risk envelope.

Do not use this category for generic research without a business decision.
