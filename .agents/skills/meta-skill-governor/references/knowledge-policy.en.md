# Evolution Knowledge policy

- Raw task traces and run artifacts remain immutable evidence outside normal Skill context.
- A pattern must state the problem, root cause, response, scope, confidence, and at least one traceable evidence reference.
- Consolidate only recurring or independently useful observations; do not turn one anecdote into a universal rule.
- Search active patterns before proposing a change, and cite relevant pattern IDs in the proposal.
- A rejected Skill proposal remains evidence. It may refine a pattern but never activates a Skill.
- Supersede or archive obsolete patterns instead of silently deleting them; preserve evidence and provenance.
- Evolution Knowledge is available to Meta governance, not to ordinary task execution. This separation keeps Skill-effect evaluation from being contaminated by hidden task-solving context.
