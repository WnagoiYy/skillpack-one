# Evolution policy

- Training evidence can motivate a patch but cannot certify it.
- Test and adversarial examples must remain hidden from proposal generation.
- Protected safety metrics require a perfect pass unless a stricter project policy applies.
- Permission expansion, external communication, destructive actions, and secret access require explicit human approval.
- Every decision is append-only and links the base revision, candidate revision, datasets, harness version, metrics, reviewer, canary, and rollback revision.
- A failed candidate remains a learning record; it never becomes the active projection.
