# Schedule a personal calendar

## Workflow

1. Confirm timezone duration participants location recurrence flexibility and authority to write.
2. Inspect relevant availability and preserve private event details.
3. Propose conflict-aware slots and obtain confirmation for material ambiguity.
4. Create only approved events then verify title time timezone participants and reminders.
