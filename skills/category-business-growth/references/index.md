# Business and growth: Atomic Skills

Improve markets, sales, marketing, finance, and business decisions.

## Atomic Skills

- `atom-analyze-customer-segments` — **Analyze customer segments**: Segment one defined customer market using traceable behavioral, needs-based, or economic evidence and state the limits of each segment.
  - Risk: `read-only`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, verify
- `atom-analyze-market` — **Analyze a market**: Produce evidence-backed findings for one commercial market question
  - Risk: `read-only`
  - Lifecycle: discover, analyze
- `atom-audit-seo` — **Audit SEO**: Audit one bounded website or content set for technical, on-page, information-architecture, and evidence-backed search opportunities without changing the site.
  - Risk: `reversible-write`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, transform
- `atom-design-pricing-strategy` — **Design a pricing strategy**: Design one pricing and packaging recommendation from value, willingness-to-pay, costs, alternatives, constraints, and testable assumptions.
  - Risk: `read-only`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, verify
- `atom-model-business-financials` — **Model business financials**: Build one transparent business financial model with explicit drivers, scenarios, unit economics, cash implications, and sensitivity checks.
  - Risk: `reversible-write`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, transform
- `atom-plan-content-strategy` — **Plan a content strategy**: Plan one coherent content program from audience needs, business outcomes, themes, channels, cadence, governance, and measurable learning loops.
  - Risk: `read-only`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, verify
- `atom-plan-product-launch` — **Plan a product launch**: Create one cross-functional go-to-market launch plan with audience, positioning, channels, readiness gates, metrics, owners, and fallback actions.
  - Risk: `reversible-write`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, transform
- `atom-write-marketing-copy` — **Write marketing copy**: Write one evidence-grounded marketing asset for a defined audience, offer, channel, and action without fabricating claims or publishing it.
  - Risk: `reversible-write`
  - Lifecycle: analyze, transform

Read only the selected Atomic Skill. Use a capability pack when the request has independently useful outcomes.
